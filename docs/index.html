TypeParkinson for iPhone and iPad

A keyboard built around your hands.

TypeParkinson watches how you touch the screen, builds a keyboard around it, and then checks — on a phrase you haven’t seen — that the new keyboard actually helps you type.

How it works

  1. Measure. Five short exercises, about three minutes. Pause or skip any of them.
  2. Build. Key size, touch interpretation, repeat handling, tap tolerance and large correction buttons — each traced to something you did.
  3. Check. Type a phrase on a standard keyboard and on yours, and see which helped.
  4. Type anywhere. Turn it on once in Settings and use it in every app.

Private by design

Your touch profile stays on your device and, if you sign in with Apple, in your own iCloud. TypeParkinson has no servers, and its keyboard runs without Full Access, so it can’t send what you type anywhere.

TypeParkinson describes how your touches behave. It isn’t a medical device, doesn’t assess or diagnose any condition, and isn’t a substitute for advice from a clinician.
