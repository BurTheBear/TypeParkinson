Every day after

Typing feels different today?

Hands aren’t the same every morning. One button on the home screen runs a six-tap check and compares today with your usual. If your touches are landing somewhere new, TypeParkinson reads them differently for the day.

The keys themselves never move. Once you approve a layout it stays put, so your fingers can learn where everything is.
